Project Overview

This project involved designing and implementing a robotic arm capable of detecting ping pong balls, predicting their trajectories, and striking them in real time. The system integrates computer vision, embedded control, kinematic modeling, and mechanical design.

System Architecture

The system is composed of the following major components:

  • Camera Module: Captures live video of the ping pong ball.
  • OpenCV Processing: Detects the ball and extracts position updates.
  • Trajectory Prediction: Fits a quadratic curve to estimate the ball's future position.
  • Servo Control: Raspberry Pi sends PWM commands to the arm using a PCA9685 driver.
  • Mechanical Arm: Custom 3–4 DOF design modeled and tested in CAD.

Trajectory Modeling

Ball positions were recorded over several frames and fit to a quadratic model:

```python # Fit quadratic curve y = ax^2 + bx + c coeffs = np.polyfit(x_positions, y_positions, 2) predicted_y = coeffs[0]*future_x**2 + coeffs[1]*future_x + coeffs[2] ```

This model leverages projectile motion and works well for predicting the strike point.
