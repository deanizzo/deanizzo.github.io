Model Wind Turbine

Collegiate Wind Competition

Overview

As Vice President and Aerodynamics team lead of the UW-Madison model wind turbine comeptition team, I have spent the past three years designing and building wind turbines. I have led a few teams including aerodynamics and mechanical where I teach students how wind turbines work and lead them through the build process. This past year I competed at the CWC in Boulder, CO.

Turbine

We made our entire turbine completely from scratch, including the blades, pitch control, generator, and electrical system. Our generator flux is an axial flux design with customer a custom stator. Our blades are made from sls deposition to balance weight and strength.
